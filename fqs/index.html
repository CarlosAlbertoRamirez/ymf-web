Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what people usually ask us.

What services do you offer?

Consultancy in Mexican Spanish training management, management training and group lessons. All of them cover speaking, listening, reading and writing, plus Mexican culture.

How do your programs fit my organization?

We start by analyzing your organization's communication needs and business goals, then customize the program so it lines up with them.

Who are your instructors?

Carlos and Denisse, both with years of experience teaching Spanish and cultural immersion, and academic backgrounds in language and education. Meet them.

Can you teach different levels in the same company?

Yes. We work with every level, from beginners to advanced, so every participant benefits regardless of where they start.

How long are the programs and how big are the groups?

It depends on your needs. We keep group sizes small enough to learn well and adjust them to the program and your preferences.

How do you measure progress?

With regular assessments and feedback, focused on how well participants use their new skills in real situations.

Is there support after the training?

Yes. We offer post-training support so your team keeps learning and applying what they practiced.

How is culture included in the classes?

Through interactive activities and real-life scenarios that show how Mexican culture and business really work.

What do I need for online classes?

A stable internet connection, a webcam and a microphone. Classes run on easy-to-use platforms.

How do we get started?

Schedule a consultation with us. We'll talk about your needs and design a training package for your organization. Contact us.

Is there a refund policy?

We don't give monetary refunds. If you're not satisfied, we can make up for it with an extra service (restrictions apply).

What if there are technical problems?

If the problem is on our side, we reschedule the full class for another day. If it's on the student's side, we'll find an arrangement depending on the circumstances.

Still have questions?

Write to us and we'll answer you personally.
