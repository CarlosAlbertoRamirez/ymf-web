Our goal: help businesses around the world through tailored Mexican Spanish training that bridges language and cultural gaps.

Meetthe crew

Carlos

Spanish teacher · Co-founder

I believe in immersive learning: real mastery comes from living the language — music, movies, books and conversations with native speakers. And above all, learning should be fun.

I've taught Spanish to foreigners for more than 5 years and trained staff at companies in the United States and Mexico in both the language and Mexican culture.

I hold a degree in Communication Science from UNAM and a master's in Government and Public Affairs, I've taught Spanish and English in Mexico, and I scored 93 on the TOEFL.

Denisse

Spanish & English teacher · Co-founder

Becoming a teacher was my dream since I was a kid. I studied English at the Escuela Normal Superior de México, CELEX and ANGLO College.

I've taught English at a secondary school in Mexico for 4 years, tutored privately for 3, and taught Spanish to foreigners for 5 — including training staff at companies in the US and Mexico.

Nothing makes me happier than watching my students grow, so I keep every class engaging, supportive and fun.
